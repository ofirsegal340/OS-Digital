# PRODUCT.md — OS Digital

register: brand

## Register
Brand. A single-page marketing landing page where the design IS the product.
Every choice serves one outcome: turn a visitor into a qualified lead.

## Users
Owners of local service businesses in Israel — cosmeticians, garages, dental and
aesthetic clinics, lawyers, trainers, local trades. Not marketing-savvy, time-poor,
on their phone, value-conscious but quality-seeking. They have been burned by cheap
"leads now" promises before. Decision driver: trust plus proof, not hype.

## Product Purpose
Convert visitors into qualified leads (contact form + WhatsApp) for OS Digital's
paid-ads management service across Facebook, Google, Instagram and TikTok.
Success = a real business owner reaches out for a consultation.

## Brand Personality
Premium and trustworthy. Calm, senior confidence — a consultant who delivers, not a
closer who shouts. Quiet authority over volume. Polished, specific, honest.
Hebrew-first, native, never translated-feeling.

## Anti-references
- The loud Israeli agency: neon yellow/red, screaming "הגדל מכירות עכשיו!!!",
  exclamation walls, urgency timers, pop-ups. This is the primary thing to avoid.
- Cheap/spammy energy: anything that reads as a get-rich scheme.
- Generic gradient SaaS template and the hero-metric cliché.

## Design Principles (strategic)
1. Proof over promises — lead with concrete specifics and real results, never adjectives.
2. Calm confidence — restraint signals premium; we never raise our voice to be heard.
3. Trust at every step — transparent process, honest claims, no dark patterns in the form.
4. One short reach to contact — the lead action is always close, never forced or hidden.
5. Hebrew-first, RTL-native — copy, rhythm and layout feel born in Hebrew.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA (>=4.5:1 body text, >=3:1 UI). RTL Hebrew native; phone input LTR.
Mobile-first (owners arrive on phones). Generous touch targets. Honors
prefers-reduced-motion. No hover-only actions. No zoom disabling.
